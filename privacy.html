Privacy Policy

ResizeFlow processes image edits locally in your browser. Images are not intentionally uploaded to a ResizeFlow server for resizing, cropping, conversion, compression, or enhancement. We do not have access to your images.

What stays local

  • Image editing and processing run in browser memory
  • Output is generated locally and downloaded by your browser
  • Metadata may be removed during canvas export

Analytics and advertising

Optional Google Analytics and AdSense help measure usage and keep the free tool available. They load only after you Accept cookies. Until then, Consent Mode keeps ad and analytics storage denied.

Third-party resources

Libraries may load from CDNs (Cropper.js, JSZip, HEIC/AVIF tools). Those providers may receive ordinary connection data such as IP address. Your image files are not sent to ResizeFlow servers for processing.

Your choices

Use Accept or Reject on the consent banner. Cookie settings in the footer can reset your choice. Essential functionality does not require advertising cookies.

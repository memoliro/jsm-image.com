Convert Images to WebP Online

WebP is a modern format that often beats JPG and PNG on file size while keeping good quality. Convert locally with ResizeFlow — no account required.

Convert to WebP

Steps

  • Upload your image (JPG, PNG, HEIC, and more)
  • Under Convert Image Format, choose WebP
  • Optionally resize and set compression quality
  • Download the WebP file

Why WebP?

Many browsers and CMSs support WebP. Smaller assets mean faster pages. If you need maximum compatibility for print or older apps, keep a JPG copy as well.

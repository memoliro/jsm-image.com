Frequently Asked Questions

Common answers regarding functionality and privacy.

Is JSM-Image.com completely free?

Yes, all single and batch editing features are 100% free with no hidden fees or sign-ups.

Are my images uploaded to any server?

No. Operations run entirely within your local browser memory via HTML5 Canvas and Web Workers.

Can I convert multiple HEIC files at once?

Yes. You can batch upload HEIC photos from iOS devices and convert them all directly into JPG, WebP, or AVIF.

Do animated GIFs keep their animation?

No. Editing runs through HTML5 Canvas, so animated GIFs are processed as a single static frame.

What is AVIF and why is it slower?

AVIF often produces files 40–50% smaller than JPEG at similar quality. Encoding runs locally via WebAssembly, which is slower than JPG or WebP but keeps processing private in your browser.

Browser compatibility: JSM-Image.com works best in current Chrome, Edge, Firefox, and Safari. HEIC/HEIF conversion and some features may depend on browser support and memory.
